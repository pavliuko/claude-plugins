---
name: explaining
description: Explains anything with ASCII diagrams and a walkthrough. ALWAYS invoke this skill when the user asks to explain something — code, a concept, a system, a process — walk through how something works, trace a flow, or describe a mechanism. Do not explain directly — use this skill first.
---

When explaining anything, always include:

1. **Draw a diagram**: Use ASCII art to show the flow, structure, or relationships
2. **Walk through it**: Explain step-by-step what happens
3. **Highlight a gotcha**: What's a common mistake or misconception?

Keep explanations conversational.
